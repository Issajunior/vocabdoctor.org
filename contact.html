Frequently Asked Questions

Is Vocab Doctor free to use? +

Yes! Vocab Doctor is free to download and use. A Pro upgrade is available for unlimited AI enrichment, advanced analytics, and priority support.

Which languages are supported? +

Vocab Doctor supports 50+ languages including Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Arabic, Chinese, Korean, Portuguese, and many more. The AI enrichment works in all supported languages.

Can I share my vocabulary lists? +

Yes! You can share any list with classmates or study partners. They'll be able to study your list and even add their own notes and enrichment.

How does the spaced repetition work? +

We use the SM-2 algorithm to schedule reviews at optimal intervals. After each card, you rate how well you remembered it (Hard / Good / Easy), and the algorithm adjusts when you'll see that card next — reviewing it just before you'd forget it.

Does my data sync across devices? +

Yes! Your vocabulary lists, progress, and streaks sync seamlessly across all your devices via secure cloud sync — available on both iOS and Android.
